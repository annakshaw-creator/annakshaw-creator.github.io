Portfolio Analysis

Bringing coherence to a data-rich financial platform

Led design for a portfolio analytics platform serving financial sales professionals—unifying two disconnected platforms into a coherent experience and defining a growth strategy for expanding to new user types.

Challenge

Built beyond its original foundation

A portfolio analytics tool for financial sales professionals—built across two separate platforms with no shared design system and no unified workflows connecting them. The challenge was to bring it together into a coherent experience while building a foundation for future growth.

Work & Outcomes

Unify, then grow

I built and led the design team, managed client relationships, and contributed hands-on to complex UX problems and strategy work throughout the engagement.

  • Design system — built a full design system for the new market intelligence section and expanded it into a unified visual language capable of bridging the legacy and new platforms
  • Feature redesigns — led design across a set of new and updated features including PDF reports, expanded navigation, first-time use experiences, and modern flexible table formatting
  • Growth strategy — at the close of the engagement, defined a framework and set of recommendations for expanding to home office advisor teams—expressed through user stories and annotated UI schematics covering new service models, expanded user controls, and bridging workflows
