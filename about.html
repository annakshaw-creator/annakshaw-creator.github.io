Complex, trust-driven product design

Fintech, financial services, and health tech make up the core of my recent work. These are environments where details matter and users need to trust what’s in front of them before they’ll act. From financial advisors managing client goals to consumers understanding their health data, I’ve developed a love for making information clear and complex workflows intuitive.

Experience thinking across the full arc

Some of the most interesting work I’ve done lives at the intersection of brand, product, and service, where no single touchpoint can answer on its own. I’ve led programs that span from brand identity through product experience, worked on physical and digital products where the two needed to feel like one thing, and contributed to cross-disciplinary service design programs that mapped experiences across every human touchpoint. It’s a lens I bring to product work, to surface connections that purely product-focused thinking misses.

Craft, judgment, and getting it right

Good design isn’t just a process or set of prompts—it’s a series of judgment calls. When to push and when to let something go. What the right level of detail is for this moment in this product. That kind of judgment is earned over time, through real work with real people on real problems. I hold a high bar not because perfection is the goal but because clarity, consistency, and restraint are what make experiences trustworthy.
